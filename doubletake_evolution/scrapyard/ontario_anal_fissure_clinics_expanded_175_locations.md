# Ontario clinics and specialists for anal-fissure assessment

**Expanded working table prepared August 4, 2026: 175 unique locations. This update adds three named non-GTA specialist practices whose physicians were not previously mentioned in the table.**

## Important interpretation notes

- **Clinic ratings** are public patient-review aggregates, not official measures of medical quality. A rating is included only when a clearly identifiable aggregate for the exact clinic was found. Ratings can change.
- **Doctor ratings** are marked **N/A** unless an unambiguous current aggregate for the exact physician was available. Similar names and outdated profiles make many doctor-rating matches unreliable.
- **Anal-fissure connection (0–10)** estimates how directly the clinic's published specialty or services relate to fissures, perianal disorders, anorectal examination or testing, colorectal surgery, anoscopy, or lower-GI care. General gastroenterology and colonoscopy-only services receive lower scores.
- **Surgery-centredness (0–10)** estimates how much the service is structurally organized around surgeons and procedures. It **does not** claim that a clinic recommends unnecessary surgery, behaves unethically, or provides poor care. There is no defensible public evidence for assigning such a probability.
- “All doctors” means all physicians identifiable from the clinic's current public roster or current public registry results checked for this working draft. Where a complete roster could not be confirmed, the table says so.
- Earlier expansions produced **172 unique locations**. This update adds **three named London specialist practices** whose physicians were absent from the table. All three are connected to colorectal or benign colorectal care. Their campuses were already represented by broad hospital-program rows, but these individual practices and physicians were not. No genuinely new Ottawa-area fissure service met the non-duplicate and verification requirements in this search.
