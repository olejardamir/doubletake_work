# Universal Job Post Template for Resume Creation — Final 10/10 Version

## Purpose

This template converts any job posting into a structured **job blueprint for resume creation**. It is intentionally **candidate-agnostic**. It describes the job first, without assuming whether any specific person is qualified.

The completed blueprint can later be paired with a candidate background file to generate a targeted resume that is accurate, ATS-aware, and grounded in real evidence.

This template is designed to work with:

- technical roles
- non-technical roles
- hybrid roles
- junior to executive roles
- contract, full-time, freelance, and internship roles
- vague or incomplete job postings
- postings with unusual titles or inconsistent wording
- postings with missing tools, metrics, seniority signals, or business context

---

# 0. Operating Rules

## 0.1 Core Principle

The template must describe **what the job requires**, not what a candidate has done.

Do not insert candidate experience, candidate skills, candidate projects, or candidate claims into this template.

## 0.2 Evidence Labels

Every important field should be labeled using one of these evidence types:

| Label | Meaning | Allowed Use |
|---|---|---|
| Directly Stated | Explicitly appears in the job posting | Safe to use as a job requirement |
| Strongly Implied | Required by the role logic, tools, domain, or responsibilities | Safe as role analysis, not as candidate claim |
| Weakly Implied | Plausible but uncertain | Use cautiously and mark uncertainty |
| Missing | Not provided in the posting | Do not invent; identify as a gap |
| Do Not Assume | Would require unsupported speculation | Exclude from resume generation unless candidate/background source confirms it |

## 0.3 Priority Labels

Use these priority levels throughout the template:

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Central to the role; resume must address it if candidate evidence exists |
| High | Important; resume should address it if truthful |
| Medium | Helpful; include only when it strengthens fit |
| Low | Contextual or optional; do not force into resume |
| Exclude | Not relevant, unsupported, or unsafe to use |

## 0.4 Resume-Safety Rules

When this blueprint is later used for resume generation:

1. Do not invent candidate experience.
2. Do not convert inferred job needs into candidate claims.
3. Do not add tools, technologies, metrics, certifications, domains, or responsibilities unless confirmed by candidate evidence.
4. Do not force every keyword into the resume.
5. Do not hide fit gaps.
6. Do not use job-posting language as proof of candidate ability.
7. Do not rewrite missing job details as facts.
8. Keep job facts, inferred needs, candidate evidence, and final resume wording separate.

# 15. Gap Handling and Inference Rules

## 15.1 Missing Information From Posting

| Missing Area | Why It Matters | How to Handle |
|---|---|---|
| Seniority unclear | Affects resume tone and scope | Infer cautiously from responsibilities and years required |
| Tools unclear | Affects ATS and skills section | Use only stated tools unless strongly implied by ecosystem |
| Metrics missing | Affects achievement bullets | Suggest metric categories, not invented numbers |
| Team structure unclear | Affects collaboration framing | Keep stakeholder references general unless stated |
| Domain unclear | Affects positioning | Use company/product context if available |
| Success criteria unclear | Affects resume summary and bullet emphasis | Infer only from role mission and repeated responsibilities |
| Required vs preferred unclear | Affects keyword priority | Treat repeated or central items as higher priority; label uncertainty |

## 15.2 Gap-Filling Rules

| Situation | Allowed Action | Prohibited Action |
|---|---|---|
| Job posting is vague | Clarify role logic and mark inferences | Present guesses as facts |
| Technology ecosystem implies missing tools | Mark as strongly or weakly implied | Insert tools into resume unless candidate confirms them |
| Metrics are not provided | Identify likely metric categories | Invent numbers |
| Seniority is not named | Infer from scope, years, autonomy, leadership | Overstate seniority |
| Business context is incomplete | Use company/product clues if available | Invent business model or customers |
| Candidate evidence is absent | Leave candidate fields blank for later matching | Assume candidate fit |

# 18. Final Resume Generation Instruction

Use this completed job blueprint to create a targeted resume only after a separate candidate-background source is provided.

The final resume should:

- align with the job's highest-priority requirements
- preserve exact job keywords where truthful
- emphasize relevant achievements over generic duties
- show measurable impact where evidence exists
- use adjacent experience only when it is accurate and clearly transferable
- avoid unsupported claims
- avoid forcing unrelated experience into the role
- clearly prioritize strongest match areas
- reduce or omit low-relevance content
- remain grounded in verified candidate experience
