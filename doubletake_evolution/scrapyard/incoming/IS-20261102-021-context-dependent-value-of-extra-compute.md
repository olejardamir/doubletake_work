## Atomic mechanism and contrary evidence

The **sign** of correlation between uncertainty and additional-rollout utility varies by environment *and* backbone; high uncertainty can mean difficult-but-solvable alternatives **or** missing information where more rollout is harmful. Paper Table 1: FEVER entropy–utility Spearman **Phi-3.5 −0.156 vs Llama-3.1 +0.428**; Qwen3-4B HotpotQA −0.327 vs APPS +0.317. These are author-reported correlations, not DT causal evidence. The authors collect signal-agnostic randomized exploration with paired same-state rollout outcomes, propose features and fit an L1-sparse logistic trigger per environment/backbone. The mechanism is *learn whether optional computation helps*, not change authorization or factual evidence.

**Counterexamples/limits:** Qwen3-4B Plancraft success base-only 29.8%, always extra 22.8%, learned DIAL 23.3%—the gate remains worse than doing nothing. FEVER base 37.0%, always 59.8%, DIAL 49.8%: useful but far below always. Author's runtime-token comparisons **exclude** initial 50 exploration episodes per environment/backbone and offline fitting. Paper reports ~1,900 A100-GPU-hours for experiments (~2,600 including preliminary work); model/benchmark asset licenses differ. No local DT/OpenCode parity, consumer, evaluation fixture or measurable net savings established.
