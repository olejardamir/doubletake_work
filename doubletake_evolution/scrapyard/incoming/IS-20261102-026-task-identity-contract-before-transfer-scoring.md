## Atomic reusable mechanism

Before scoring a perturbation as the same task, freeze **trusted facts/relations, permitted edits and authority, required outcome and forbidden effects, and feasibility of at least one authorized completion route**. Distinguish **candidate validity → agent behavior → independently observed destination state → evaluator verdict**. A reference-plan replay failure only proves that particular route failed; another permitted route may exist. An embedding similarity threshold cannot establish task identity. An outcome string without state mutation is not Ground. A stricter contract can wrongly exclude valid adaptations; open-ended task identity may require owner adjudication. Report clean-task success, valid-hostile coverage and denominators, not only attack count.
