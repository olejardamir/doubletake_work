## Source findings versus DT inference
- **Source fact:** FLARE derives expected **agent relationships, termination patterns, task execution and tool calls** from an agent application, then explores intra-agent behavior and legal inter-agent paths with coverage-guided bounded mutations. It converts traces into semantic events and checks failures against expected behavior. Its core discriminant is *functional coordination/termination failure versus ordinary crash or syntactic coverage*.
- **Source evidence (authors only):** In 16 AutoGen applications, reported inter-agent coverage was 96.9% versus 87.4% for a baseline; 61 failures included 56 non-crash functional failures. Reported termination classes included unmet termination conditions and exceeding maximum rounds. These are **not** DT outcomes or independent replications. Baseline comparability is limited by some missing environmental runs; standard intra-agent coverage showed little advantage. Some LLM-extracted specifications were wrong; the authors report 61/64 components aligned with human ground truth. The implementation is AutoGen-specific; extension to DT is **unverified**.
- **Independent frontier cross-check:** Siddiqui, van der Aalst & Schuster, *Computing Alignments for Partially-ordered Traces Through Petri Net Unfoldings*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00550 (2025), directly examined abstract/introduction. Concurrency can make strict timestamp-order trace alignment misleading. This is **contextual caution**, not evidence FLARE implements partial-order alignments; do not conflate the two.
- **DT-specific inference:** A safe *semantic termination oracle* could help classify **why** a run fails to complete despite successful intermediate steps, distinguishing authorized fail-closed integrity refusal, premature success claims, missing final transition, loop after task completion and stale/replayed receipts. This does **not** resolve the known B1 owner-decision block, produce an authenticated mission-consumer receipt, or authorize recovery. It must not relabel expected integrity refusal as a bug to be bypassed.
