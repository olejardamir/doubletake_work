## Mechanism and boundaries

- Fix application/owner-approved persistent outcome multiset **A** before an authorized execution; independently observe actual persistent effects **E** at a certified coverage/attribution boundary. Only complete observation with **E=A** is **EXACT** and eligible to continue; complete mismatch is **DIVERGED**; missing outlet/uncertain attribution is **INDETERMINATE**, never silent success.
- Extra, missing and duplicate effects count even if the requested tool call returned success. Approval, effect and durable continuation must bind to the same occurrence/revision; stale/replayed approval must fail. Staged/promotable backends can withhold commit; an opaque external service's already-realized effects cannot be undone by readback, so block *dependent continuation* rather than claim rollback.
- **Critical counterexample:** unregistered reachable persistence outlet makes a claimed complete effect-set unreliable. Independent owner approval and complete observer coverage are assumptions, not guarantees. A GitHub issue-field readback cannot attribute unrelated out-of-band edits to the evaluated request.
- Authors report 206-task reference comparisons with no incorrect commits and 618/618 clean executions retained; independently approved task subset was much smaller (three PostgreSQL and twelve business tasks); 18 local-task comparisons report 4.35x median held/direct runtime ratio and 1.08 ms median added time. These are author-reported, setup-specific, **not DT measurements**. No independent replication or licensed code import.
- Contrast, not a second candidate: *CASEL* arXiv:2610.36570 (2026-10-27), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36570 , assumes intervention effectiveness in simulation and cannot establish DT deployment safety.
