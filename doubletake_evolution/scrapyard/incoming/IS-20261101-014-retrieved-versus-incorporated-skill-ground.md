## Distinct, testable finding

The paper separates **(R) retrieval** of a relevant skill, **(I) incorporation/loading** of that skill into the active solver, and **(A) application** that actually changes task performance. SRA-Bench comprises 5,400 instances, 636 hand-built gold skills and 25,626 distractor skills in a 26,262-skill corpus. The authors report that skill-loading rates often vary weakly with gold-skill availability, and agents may load skills even when unnecessary. For example, their BM25 top-50 comparison reports Qwen3-4B loading at 21.8% with a gold skill present versus 16.9% absent; these are **paper-specific model/benchmark observations**, not DT estimates. Retrieval success, a knowledge-store entry, a mission receipt, or prompt visibility alone cannot prove incorporation or useful application.

**Potential novelty versus existing Scout work:** IS-009 already tests *per-consumer negative transfer after a skill is supplied*; IS-006 already tests *source ablation and causal downstream Ground*. This item instead isolates the **intermediate selection/loading gate**: a qualified source can be discovered and approved yet never actually loaded, or irrelevant material can be loaded without justified need. Existing DT source/tests might already cover it; **current implementation parity and owner are UNKNOWN**. Do not create a new skill store, evaluator, loader, or mission task merely because the paper has a benchmark.
