## Mechanism and negative evidence

SWE-Prometheus compares a **fixed base repository** and an independently reconstructed **treated repository** under the same clean-environment probes, retains commands/exit codes/logs/tool versions/timeouts and missing-evidence statuses, applies behavior-preservation gates, and uses no-op and mechanical-template controls to expose credit for superficial governance artifacts. It distinguishes unavailable evidence, infrastructure failure, invalid patch, behavior regression, behavior preserved with no improvement, and verified improvement. Independent same-evidence judgments are used for rubric-dependent dimensions; evidence quality and gate strength are reported separately.

Authors report 60 repositories (22 shared public instances), and a repository-blind template obtaining mean normalized governance improvement 0.272 on a ten-repository auxiliary set while failing to improve two harder dimensions. A template-generated placeholder test could pass despite separate lint failures. In a no-op condition the score standard deviation was 0.073. Crucially, **only 24/60 behavior gates had mutation-demonstrated discriminative power**; measured breakage varied with gate strength. One rollout per public model-instance pair, scaffold differences, subjective judge variability, non-random sample, and possible training contamination limit transfer. The paper explicitly warns that adding tests/configuration is not equivalent to execution-backed benefit.
