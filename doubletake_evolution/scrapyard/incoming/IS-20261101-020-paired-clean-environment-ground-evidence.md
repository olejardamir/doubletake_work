## Mechanism and counterevidence

SWE-Prometheus evaluates fixed pre-change and treated repository states with **matched clean-environment commands**, actual test/build/configuration evidence, behavior-preservation gates, independent evidence judgments, and explicit `UNAVAILABLE`/infrastructure/invalid/regressed/no-improvement/improved verdicts. No-op, template and mutation controls distinguish an actual behavioral gain from adding tests/docs/configuration that look like progress.

**Author-reported (not DT results):** 60 repositories; ten models evaluated on 22 shared public instances; reported normalized improvement 0.0568–0.5760 and breakage 0–23%; repository-blind template mean 0.272 on ten-repo auxiliary batch with no gains in reproducible environment or dependency/security; no-op score SD 0.073. Reported gate-strength breakdown: 24/60 mutation-detected, 27 blind, 3 vacuous, 6 absent. Thus a green test or governance score alone may be non-discriminating. Single rollout per public model-instance pair, scaffold differences, imperfect gates, judge variability, nonrandom sample and contamination limit inference. No paper score is a DT acceptance threshold.
