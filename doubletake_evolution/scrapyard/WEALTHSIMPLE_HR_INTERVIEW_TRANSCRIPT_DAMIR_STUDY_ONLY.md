# D. The book-of-record project in simple English

## 36. What is a book of record?

It is the trusted source that says what money, balances, borrowing, fees, and repayments officially exist. Other systems may display or use the information, but the book of record must preserve the accurate history.

## 37. What problem did your system solve?

It created one reliable place to record financial activity and compare it with information from other systems. That reduced uncertainty, made recovery safer, and helped teams find differences faster.

## 39. What products did it support?

It supported balances, borrowing, repayments, interest, fees, collateral, buying power, and margin-related decisions.

## 40. What does double-entry mean?

Every financial change is recorded in two matching parts. This makes it easier to prove that money has not appeared or disappeared without an explanation.

## 41. What is reconciliation?

Reconciliation means comparing two records that should agree. If they do not agree, the system identifies the difference so it can be explained or corrected.

## 42. What is buying power?

Buying power is the amount a client can safely use for a purchase or investment after considering cash, positions, borrowing, and the rules that limit risk.

## 43. What is collateral?

Collateral is an asset that supports a loan. Its value and eligibility help determine how much a client can borrow safely.

## 44. What is margin?

Margin allows a client to invest using borrowed funds. The system must continually check that the account has enough value to support the borrowing.

# 5. Words and habits to avoid

Avoid:

- “Basically” at the start of every answer.
- “And so on.”
- “Whatever.”
- “Things of that nature.”
- “Maybe.”
- “I do not remember” when the information is in the job posting.
- Long explanations of tools before stating the result.
- Criticizing large companies, small companies, managers, or former colleagues.
- Calling AI output “garbage.”
- Saying that a current or previous employer made poor technology choices.
- Giving a minimum salary before hearing the published range.
- Giving current compensation unless there is a clear reason to do so.
- Claiming ownership of work completed by the whole team.
- Turning an HR answer into a system-design lecture.

Prefer:

- “The purpose was…”
- “My responsibility was…”
- “The result was…”
- “The main risk was…”
- “I worked with…”
- “I would be happy to explain the technical detail in the next interview.”
