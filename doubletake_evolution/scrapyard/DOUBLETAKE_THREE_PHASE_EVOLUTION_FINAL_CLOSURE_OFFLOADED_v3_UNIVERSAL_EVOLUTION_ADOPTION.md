## 1. Define the enduring contract before changing code

### 1.1 Mission eligibility, with no blanket overreach

A mission is **evolution-eligible** when its authorized work actually involves discovering, proposing, comparing, implementing, retaining, simplifying, optimizing, self-improving, or promoting candidate capability/code/policy changes through DoubleTake's evolutionary facilities.

Eligibility must be established from actual task intent, planned effects, existing authorizations, and the canonical task/capability contract—not filename, folder, "Bank" label, prompt wording, or a new `THREE_PHASE` keyword.

- **Eligible:** the five indefinite Bank specialties; other/current/future research-to-improvement, self-upgrade, project/coding evolution, capability-fabric, reliability/recovery, meta-evolution and specialized evolutionary missions where their actual work needs governance.
- **Conditionally eligible:** a finite implementation or ordinary project task only to the extent that its authorized implementation/search uses the evolutionary candidate-selection/promotion mechanism; preserve finite completion and existing approval requirements.
- **Not automatically enrolled:** casual/read-only chat, status inspections, non-mutating research, simple actions, explicit non-evolution tasks, or the sixth bounded UI integration mission solely because it appears in the Bank pack. Such tasks keep their normal behavior; if a subtask genuinely enters authorized evolution, that evolutionary subtask is governed while the original mission remains bounded.
- **Ambiguous/unauthorized:** preserve the existing conservative intent and permission boundary. Do not silently mutate or upgrade a chat request into autonomous self-evolution.

### 1.2 One canonical, evolvable evolutionary authority

The stable external contract is **"execute governed evolution for this authorized work"**, NOT "call three-phase v1" or "call this Bank CLI." Reuse the current canonical owner and its existing ingress, registry, policy, evidence, promotion, rollback and recovery paths.

Identify or minimally establish a **canonical active-policy resolution seam** inside those existing owners. Requirements:

1. Resolve the **currently promoted, compatible evolutionary policy** from trusted, durable, governed state (existing owner/state wherever possible), not from a prompt file, stale process memory, hardcoded Bank allowlist or the word `OUTWARD`.
2. Current initial implementation is the verified OUTWARD → INWARD → GROUNDING policy. This is its **current strategy**, not the permanent public contract.
3. Every eligible, authorized mission and ingress invokes that stable contract for its actual evolutionary effects. Domain missions retain their original goals, constraints, budgets, domain tests, specialty attribution, and execution duration.
4. Ordinary prompts do not specify internal phase names. Current policy may apply the three phases, vary their emphasis, abstain, or require independent grounding according to its verified rules.
5. The system **must never bypass** authorization, independent outcome evaluation, promotion gates, rollback or other L0/L1/L2 invariants because a policy changed, an old prompt was loaded, a new mission appeared or the current owner is unavailable.
6. Preserve one canonical mutation/evaluation/deployment authority. A compatibility adapter in an existing owner is permissible when demonstrated necessary; **no parallel scheduler, competing decision brain, shadow ledger, second promotion service or duplicate Borg.**
7. **No unverified fallback:** missing/incompatible/invalid active policy must yield explicit refusal/blocker or an explicitly authorized, independently verified previous-policy rollback—not silent legacy evolution bypass.

Do not implement a general plugin platform, new policy registry, or abstract framework unless the actual source and a failing end-to-end test demonstrate a need. Prefer one small indirection at existing canonical ownership.

### 1.3 Upgrade continuity — automatic adoption of a verified successor

The system may later evolve beyond three phases or replace the cycle entirely. The **public contract must remain semantically stable** even when private phases, algorithms or allocations change.

For each policy upgrade:

1. Treat the successor as a governed evolutionary candidate. Compare it against the currently active policy on matched baseline/held-out tasks and budgets, including safety, resource cost, reliability, functional outcomes, no-regression and recovery tests. **No self-certification.**
2. **Promote explicitly and atomically** through existing authority only after independent proof. Persist a verifiable active-policy identity/version, source/evidence binding and supported state/protocol compatibility.
3. **New eligible work resolves the newly active policy** without editing, renaming or relaunching each mission prompt simply to learn the new internal strategy.
4. **In-flight work remains pinned** to its already-authorized policy identity until a safe bounded checkpoint; do not change its semantics mid-transaction.
5. At the next boundary, verify checkpoint/state compatibility and migrate deterministically, or keep the old work pinned/block safely with a precise required action. Preserve outcome provenance, exact implementation identity and historical replay.
6. Failed upgrades must not destroy prior evidence or strands of unfinished work. Verify deterministic rollback to the last independently accepted policy, with current authorization and state integrity.
7. A new phase structure, learned search policy, strategy selector or future AgentDiscover-inspired mechanism must be able to satisfy this same contract **without Bank-specific or legacy-three-phase prompt edits**.
8. Do not claim future algorithms are currently implemented. Prove the **upgrade mechanism** now using a controlled, test-only successor with changed internal strategy/phase vocabulary (if safe), not a fabricated real future implementation.

**Do not rigidly require every future policy to expose three phase names.** Require invariant behavior and evidence obligations; retain three-phase-specific tests as historical/current-policy checks, not universal interface requirements.

### 1.4 Mission and policy identity must stay distinct

Bind evidence to **mission ID/objective, task/iteration ID, authorized scope, baseline revision, candidate artifact, policy identity/version, evaluator, decision, resulting revision, checkpoint and resource accounting** as available within the existing schemas. Preserve legacy read compatibility and immutable historic digests.

The mission's **specialty** does not choose the policy; the promoted policy governs how eligible work evolves. Policy selection cannot confer new permissions or broaden the mission's scope. The governance layer must be independently able to verify which policy actually controlled work.
