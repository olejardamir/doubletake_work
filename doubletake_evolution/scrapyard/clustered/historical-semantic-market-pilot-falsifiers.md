## Distinct mechanisms and counterevidence

1. **Prevent self-match/leakage** [SP-01]: fit baseline and semantic target only from a frozen source universe, then rank unseen holdouts; score source members leave-one-out. July COTI ranked #1 (0.178) without COTI in synthesis, but this proves only a narrow TF-IDF mechanics case, not prediction.
2. **Anti-dominance can become over-filtering** [SP-01 vs SP-02]: July multi-winner prose support removed ETH paraphrase, but February single high-return KITE agent theme was removed by the same strict consensus filter. February vector ranked unseen VVV #1 (0.189; reported ~+96%), while consensus prose selected COTI (~−14.22%). Distinguish multi-source consensus, economically strong single-source *hypothesis*, and weak project-specific noise. Do not assume prose and numeric vectors are interchangeable.
3. **Strong same-month matches do not imply future prediction** [SP-03 vs SP-04]: April unseen EUL #1 (+50%) while SPK #12 gained +70.88%, a clear missed winner. Jan 16–31 → Feb VVV #1 was a retrospective forward-lag observation with February already seen by researchers; reported VVV return ranges +90–131% by provider and Spearman n=19 ≈+0.43 (p≈0.069), not pristine OOS.
4. **Retrospective model tuning can fail next holdout** [SP-05]: multi-vector/channel design retrospectively recovered April SPK #1 (+70.88%) but frozen May top-5 returned −3.46% vs pool +0.12%, and INJ +91.20% ranked ~#16; Spearman ≈−0.055, p≈0.83. Preserve this **negative result**, do not report April success as generalized predictive improvement.

## Transferable DT research hygiene (conditional, not new mission)

The reusable abstraction is **freeze source universe and transformations before holdout, preserve negative months, distinguish mechanism fit from unseen generalization, compare against simple baselines at matched budget, and never optimize acceptance after seeing outcomes**. Potential relevance to DT external-knowledge evaluation and held-out transfer tests is generic; Director's current B1/B7 authorization/receipt gaps are not addressed. Reproduction requires original dated project documents, price source/convention, exact vectorizer/embedding, point-in-time universes, complete candidate list, frozen parameters and independent oracle. **Unknown:** underlying raw market data and documents, exact price-series parity, survivorship, pre-registration, independent replication, current DT parity. Do not promote a new RESEARCH_OFFLOAD proposal without a concrete DT-specific failure and authorized experiment.
