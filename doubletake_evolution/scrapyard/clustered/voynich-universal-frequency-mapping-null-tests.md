## Common experimental contract (defensible shared substance)

1. **Input/provenance:** fix manuscript transcription convention and exact edition (e.g., Takahashi EVA and independently sourced alternative); preserve folio, page, section, glyph/token uncertainty, token frequencies, global/page rankings and source digests. No original manuscript interpretation is assumed. Test robustness across transcription variants and manuscript sections.
2. **Reference hypothesis:** select, license and version historically plausible language corpora (medieval Latin, early Italian/German, justified Hebrew, etc.) and/or *explicitly provisional* English translations of herbal, medical, botanical, recipe, pharmaceutical, astrological/alchemical texts. Clean and optionally lemmatize; retain corpus topic/section metadata, stopwords, frequency ranks, word/lemma embeddings and topic vectors. English proof-of-concept is **not** historical-language identification; embedding model must support chosen language (modern English vectors may distort archaic Latin/German).
3. **Global mapping/search:** one deterministic token→word/lemma assignment per Voynich token across the **entire** manuscript. Genetic algorithm individuals are complete mappings, not page-specific dictionaries. Use frequency-compatible initialization, mutation, crossover, elitism, multiple seeds, fixed budget and independent train/validation/held-out pages (illustrative 80/20 split only, not a guarantee). Avoid leaking held-out pages into selection or optimizing each page separately. Store full dictionary, seed, corpus, model and generation history for reproducibility.
4. **Semantic score:** map each page to a bag of content words, exclude stopwords from semantic coherence while retaining raw counts for frequency modeling. Precompute word embeddings outside the GA; average word vectors into page vector; compare to topic vectors (VF-R suggests mean cosine similarity to top three topics) and subtract matched same-size random-word baseline. Measure within-page coherence, topic alignment, within-/between-section separation and stability of repeated Voynich tokens/phrases; **do not require grammatical sentences or modern word order**. VF-R explicitly rejects reference-language bigram/trigram fluency as invalid proxy; VF-U included only a *weak* n-gram plausibility component. Repeated source-token sequence stability is a safer shared control than grammatical fluency.
5. **Matched nulls:** frequency-compatible assignment shuffles, random same-size reference word sets, wrong corpora/sections, page permutations and **shuffled manuscript token order with the full GA rerun at the same budget**. Reoptimizing nulls is essential; comparing an optimized real score against unoptimized nulls falsely inflates evidence. Keep random seeds, null distributions, component score means/SD, empirical tail probabilities, train/held-out deltas, transcription/corpus/model sensitivity, negative and null results. Do not equate a large arbitrary z-score or apparent topic with a real translation.
6. **Resource limits:** CPU for tokenization, rank scoring, GA, nulls and aggregation; GPU (historical RTX 3060 Ti/8 GB example) for one-time embedding generation and optional sparse final inspection. Cache embeddings; no embedding/LLM calls inside the per-candidate GA loop. Historic runtime guesses are not measured benchmarks.
7. **Reporting:** for each ranked mapping, report full dictionary/revision, per-component and baseline-adjusted scores, strongest/weakest pages, topic/section profiles, repeated motifs, seed stability, matched nulls, held-out generalization and corpus/transcription sensitivity. Optional qualitative LLM output must be segregated from statistical evidence. Failures constrain only this mapping/corpus/search hypothesis, not every Voynich explanation.

## Negative controls, falsification and unknowns

**Critical fails:** page-specific dictionary, holdout contamination, scoring fluent English as meaning, ignoring shuffled-full-pipeline null, wrong-corpus equal gains, optimization-only effect, nonrepeatable across seeds/transcriptions, or mislabeling a semantic cloud as decipherment. If real vs matched-null improvement disappears on held-out pages, conclude **no signal for this tested hypothesis**. An above-null stable score only supports *a weak corpus-dependent semantic association* warranting independent expert validation; no historical translation claim.

**UNKNOWN:** original author/date of variants; exact manuscript/transcription source, license and quality; corpus rights and sampling bias; model embeddings for historical language; GA compute/stop rules and variance; empirical tests, null scores and statistical calibration; independent domain-expert review. A proper future study requires frozen inputs, pre-registered hypotheses, equal-budget nulls, independent holdouts and reproducible negative results.

**DT applicability:** at most indirect test-design discipline (fixed search budget, adversarial nulls, provenance and no proxy-to-Ground leap). Existing DT governance already requires independent Ground; there is no demonstrated novelty or authorized assimilation path. **No RESEARCH_OFFLOAD proposal** for this historical non-critical topic. Revisit only upon a Director-identified matching DT exploration failure with source-pinned baseline and permitted test.
