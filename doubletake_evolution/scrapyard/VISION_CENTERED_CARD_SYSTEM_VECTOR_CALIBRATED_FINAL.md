Each physical card has two semantic states. Hearts and Diamonds are **red primary / black mirrored**; Spades and Clubs are **black primary / red mirrored**.

## Center

### Vision

Vision is the organized capacity by which a field becomes available to seeing, selection, and interpretation. It sits between openness and exclusion, motion and fixation, clarity and distortion: enough receptivity to register what is present, enough focus to distinguish figure from background, and enough continuity to maintain orientation as attention shifts. From this center, seeing can sharpen, diffuse, expand, narrow, stabilize, wander, reveal, or disappear. Vision is therefore not a particular image but the central condition from which different modes of perception become possible.

## Methodological core

This document is a semantic transport of the rebalanced 104-state card geometry into the domain of visual perception. The center of the system has been moved to **Vision**, while the relative card coordinates, same-card mirror relationships, Direct Opposites, and the two-arc circular topology are preserved. The resulting states are not claimed to be a scientific taxonomy of vision; they are a conceptual system organized around seeing, attention, visual organization, and perceptual change.

For semantic auditing, the canonical embedding text for each state is **only its definition plus Foundation principles**. The Foundation principles are intentionally state-specific: they carry the rank and suit tendencies in natural language without repeating identical axis boilerplate across the deck. Card names, coordinate labels, Toward sections, Mirroring sections, Direct Opposites, arc labels, and structural metadata are excluded from the embedding input. This prevents the declared geometry from being trivially copied into the vectors instead of being expressed by the meanings themselves.

### Relationship operators

- **Same-card mirror:** the reflected state of the same physical card across the Vision center. A mirror pair is treated as an approximate **diameter through the center**, not as a neighboring step on the circular path.
- **Direct Opposite:** the centrally opposite coordinate within the same primary or mirrored surface. It is a separate relation from same-card mirroring.
- **Arc neighbor:** the previous or next state on one of the two semantic arcs that together form the circle.
- **Grid neighbor:** an orthogonally adjacent state on the underlying 13 × 4 surface.

### Full circle

The first half of the circle traverses the primary 13 × 4 surface from **A♥ Refocusing** to **K♣ Blindness**. The second half begins at **A♥ Defocusing**, the mirror of the first primary state, and traverses the reflected surface in the **same card order** until **K♣ Magnification**. The circle then closes back toward Refocusing. This ordering is required by the center geometry: if corresponding primary and mirrored states are joined by straight lines, those mirror lines act as approximate diameters, so mirrored counterparts must not be treated as adjacent points on the circumference.

## Joker

The Joker represents the act of seeing the system as a whole rather than occupying one visual state. It integrates field, focus, context, movement, persistence, interruption, clarity, and uncertainty. In a reading, the Joker asks how the observer is organizing the act of seeing itself: what is being selected, what is being excluded, and how the current visual or interpretive frame might change.

## A♥

### Primary — Refocusing (red A♥)

Refocusing is the deliberate redirection of seeing after attention has settled elsewhere. It changes what occupies the perceptual center without requiring the entire visual field to change. A newly dominant local feature or region can therefore reorganize attention around a fresh center.

**Foundation principles:** Refocusing combines fresh reconfiguration before an arrangement settles with active attention, intensity, motion, or directed visual effort. Refocusing realizes this active change by displacing one attentional center with another, so movement in the field appears as directed selection rather than diffuse instability.

**Toward Reframing:** Refocusing moves toward **Reframing** when its present organization begins to give way to the next visual condition. Reframing is a change in the interpretive frame through which the same visual material is organized.

**Mirroring toward Defocusing:** Across the Vision center, **Refocusing** reflects to **Defocusing**. This is a same-card diameter relation rather than an adjacency on the circular path.

**Direct Opposite:** **K♣ Blindness**

**Structural metadata — exclude from embedding text:**
- Coordinate: **Change × active perceptual energy**
- Arc: **Magnification → Refocusing → Reframing**
- Same-card mirror: **Defocusing**
- Direct Opposite: **K♣ Blindness**

### Mirrored — Defocusing (black A♥)

Defocusing is the loosening of a previously selected focal point so that boundaries soften and peripheral information becomes more available. It reduces commitment to one visual target without eliminating perception itself. At its broadest, the release of focal structure approaches an undifferentiated field from which another center may later emerge.

**Foundation principles:** Defocusing combines minimal boundary commitment with maximal availability of alternatives with loosening, dispersal, interruption, or reversal of established selection. Defocusing realizes reflected disruption by releasing the dominance of one focal center; what is loosened becomes available to a wider, less bounded field rather than disappearing outright.

**Toward Focus:** Defocusing moves toward **Focus** when its present organization begins to give way to the next visual condition. Focus is the concentration of perceptual resources on a selected region or feature.

**Mirroring toward Refocusing:** Across the Vision center, **Defocusing** reflects to **Refocusing**. This is a same-card diameter relation rather than an adjacency on the circular path.

**Direct Opposite:** **K♣ Magnification**

**Structural metadata — exclude from embedding text:**

- Mirrored arc: **Blindness → Defocusing → Focus**

- Direct Opposite: **K♣ Magnification**
