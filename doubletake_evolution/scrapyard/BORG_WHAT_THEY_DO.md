# What the Borg Do

# 1. Master Borg Operating Loop

1. Observe the external environment.
2. Observe the internal condition of the Collective.
3. Detect new entities, species, vessels, systems, signals, events, anomalies, opportunities, threats, failures, and capability gaps.
4. Classify what was detected.
5. Determine whether it is relevant.
6. Estimate its threat.
7. Estimate its novelty.
8. Estimate its usefulness.
9. Estimate its distinctiveness.
10. Estimate its resistance or assimilation difficulty.
11. Estimate the cost and risk of acting on it.
12. Update its priority.
13. Decide whether to ignore, observe, investigate, communicate, cooperate, exploit, assimilate, adapt to, evade, quarantine, or destroy it.
14. Allocate drones, vessels, processing capacity, and other resources.
15. Investigate important problems in parallel.
16. Acquire useful information and capability from outside the Collective when advantageous.
17. Invent or engineer internally when external acquisition is unavailable or insufficient.
18. Adapt to new threats, failures, and countermeasures.
19. Execute the selected action.
20. Measure the result.
21. Learn from success and failure.
22. Propagate useful validated knowledge and adaptations.
23. Repair damage and degradation.
24. Isolate contamination or dangerous divergence.
25. Salvage useful information, components, and capability from failures.
26. Synchronize distributed state.
27. Update the shared world model.
28. Update the capability model.
29. Recompute priorities and capability gaps.
30. Repeat indefinitely.
