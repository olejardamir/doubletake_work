1. \(P\) is required to state or prove a substantive constructibility theorem;

   by established mathematics;

   existing algebraic or order structure;

That statement is a novelty result in the methodological sense only.

It is not a claim of new mathematics.

# 109. R-RED-007 — Indexed-Frame Collapse Theorem

## Theorem

Let \(\mathcal C:\mathcal F\to\mathbf{Cat}\) be a formally specified indexed constructor system, \((f_0,x_0)\) an initial total state, and \(\mathcal G\) a set of successful objects of \(\int_{\mathcal F}\mathcal C\).

A finite construction that may alternate arbitrarily between within-frame construction steps and admissible frame changes exists from \((f_0,x_0)\) to \(\mathcal G\) if and only if a goal object is reachable from \((f_0,x_0)\) in the underlying directed graph of

\[
\int_{\mathcal F}\mathcal C.
\]

## Proof

Every within-frame step is a morphism lying over an identity frame morphism. Every frame-changing construction step is, by definition, a morphism of the Grothendieck total category. Hence every finite mixed construction sequence is a finite composable sequence of total morphisms and therefore a directed path.

Conversely, every directed path in the total category decomposes into precisely the frame morphisms and fibre morphisms that define the indexed construction process.

\[
\square
\]

# 118. The frame-invention dichotomy
