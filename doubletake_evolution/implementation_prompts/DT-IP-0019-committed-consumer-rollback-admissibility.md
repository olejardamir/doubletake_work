## Distinct proposed behavior
A checkpoint that is structurally restorable can be **semantically unsafe** after downstream consumers commit dependent results or irreversible effects occur. At a *real existing authorized rollback-decision seam*, consider a **read-only conservative admissibility discriminator**, not a new rollback controller: match exact failed instance and re-entry ordinal; validate certified checkpoint boundary, read/write dependency edges, committed consumer lineage, and effect/irreversibility policy. A dependent committed consumer, irreversible effect, wrong instance, missing edge, ambiguous provenance or absent contract **vetoes local rollback**. Such a veto does not itself prohibit other owner-authorized safe recovery; **whole-task replay is not automatically safe** with external effects. Never mutate protected history or infer that a synthetic file rollback establishes cross-consumer semantics.
