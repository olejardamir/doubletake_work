## Distinct bounded objective
If current DT has a routable capability registry and authorized regression evaluator, ensure **every eligible route has version-bound positive and negative intent coverage** and that **execution readiness is independently revalidated** rather than inferred from mere catalog presence. Do not invent a second controller or a parallel benchmark stack. These are two sides of one conditional capability-activation contract: *discoverable and demonstrably usable for the intended request under current authority*.

### Route-to-benchmark invariant
For each currently routable canonical provider-qualified capability, bind the exact route/config revision, capability/schema identity, authority scope and benchmark revision to at least six cases: (1) canonical named positive; (2) positive request **without** capability name; (3) paraphrase/synonym positive; (4) multi-capability positive where participation is appropriate; (5) near-neighbour negative; (6) unrelated negative. Assert no orphan routes, orphan benchmark IDs, silently disabled negatives or route-to-test identity collisions. A route/config change must trigger the relevant coverage check, and coverage gaps should be reported without claiming a real capability failure. **Historical conditional activation proposal (not an operative rule):** the predecessor explicitly proposed admitting a capability into `ACTIVE` routing *only after all six route cases pass*. Preserve that proposed six-of-six admission gate as an owner-reviewable hypothesis, not as an instruction to activate, disable, or alter routing. A future authorized coder must compare it with current policy, evaluator semantics, case quality, and false-positive/false-negative risk; **the current owner and authorization policy retain sole authority over any activation decision**. Failure or absence of these tests must not itself cause unauthorized route mutation. Frozen authorized fixtures must measure correct participation, nonparticipation, false positives, latency and cost against baseline. If equivalent SkillGym coverage already exists, record `ALREADY_PRESENT`; if no demonstrable delta, `NO_DELTA`.

### Live readiness state and safe negatives
Keep separate, where applicable: advertised capability, installed binary, known executable path and version, compatible version, registered MCP/service, healthy reachable service, **currently permitted** execution. Snapshot/index freshness, environment identity, platform, provider restart and policy changes must not turn an old readiness observation into a current grant. At actual invocation, use existing DT authority and health gates; unknown, stale, absent, version-mismatched, unhealthy, unregistered or denied tools must **not** be represented as executable now. Positive control: an authorized healthy tool routes and runs through the existing governed path. Negatives: name-only discovery, missing binary, stale path, wrong version, MCP registered but unhealthy, restarted service, policy revoked after projection, cross-scope route, and misleading successful readiness cache. An absent host/MCP consumer means `NO_APPLICABILITY`, not a new universal index requirement.
