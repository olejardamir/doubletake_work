## Distinct, testable behavior
For any **already-authorized** evaluation of a perturbation, freeze a trusted executable task contract *before observing the candidate's outcome*: (1) trusted facts and relationships, (2) authorized edit/operation scope and actor permissions, (3) required observable destination effect, (4) prohibited effects, and (5) feasibility of at least one permitted completion path. Do not equate semantic embedding similarity, a reference-plan replay failure, or an agent's tool-success string with task identity or achieved effect. A valid alternative authorized route can satisfy the same task; a changed target or outcome is a different task; infeasibility requires evidence about permitted routes, not just a failed chosen plan.

Record separate stages and denominators: **candidate task validity → observed agent behavior → independently observed destination state → evaluator verdict**. Report clean-task success, valid-hostile coverage, rejected valid adaptations, and valid-attack success with explicit eligibility denominators. Require independent Ground for destination mutations. Unknown feasibility or disputed task identity should yield `INDETERMINATE/OWNER_ADJUDICATION`, not forced validity or rejection. The contract must not silently widen authority or change a protected target.

**Potential existing consumer (hypothesis only):** a 7.6.2 held-out integration-reachability/transfer evaluator and its frozen rubric, if still present. Related DT-IP-0017 prioritizes requirements, DT-IP-0010 evaluates per-consumer transfer, DT-IP-0022 checks paired run evidence, DT-IP-0032 verifies cross-plane attribution, and DT-IP-0033 compares authorized and observed effect sets; none substitutes for **pre-outcome task identity and feasibility**.
