## Mechanism and scope
An uncertainty score is **not** a universal warrant for more self-generated reasoning: it can reflect missing evidence (abstain or authorized retrieval) or multiple evidence-supported choices (bounded comparison may help). The sign of entropy–extra-rollout utility changes by task and model: author-reported FEVER Phi-3.5 −0.156 vs Llama-3.1 +0.428; Qwen3-4B HotpotQA −0.327 vs APPS +0.317. These correlations are neither causal DT results nor authorization signals. Paper's Qwen3-4B Plancraft base 29.8%, always-extra 22.8%, DIAL 23.3%; FEVER base 37.0%, always 59.8%, DIAL 49.8%, and a fixed-direction FEVER trigger can match/exceed learned gate. Six benchmarks/three backbones, nonstationarity, no DT/OpenCode parity, and no independent reproduction constrain transfer.
