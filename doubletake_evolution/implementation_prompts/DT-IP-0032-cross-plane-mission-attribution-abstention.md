## Distinct mechanism and ownership hypotheses

A declared agent/tool action, an OS-observed process/file/socket event, and an independently verified destination effect are **different evidence planes**. Time-nearby observations do not establish causal attribution. Preserve the observation source, agent identity claim, observation trust, command/process correlation method, causal-vs-inferred status, exact revision, outcome and missing evidence as separate fields or verdict dimensions. A heuristic command→process match is **INFERRED_NON_CAUSAL**, not authenticated execution; zero or multiple compatible processes require abstention. A singleton match can also be a false attribution when the true process was missed and an impostor was observed. A file-open event without exact content verification is `READ_BYTES_UNVERIFIED`; opening a file does not establish decision-use of its bytes. Session correlation alone does not identify the acting agent. Shared identifiers aid correlation but do not alone prove causation.

Hypothesized consumer: existing B7 mission-consumption evaluator/assessor for `ACTIVE_MISSION.md`, not a new daemon, telemetry collector, signer, proof writer, dispatcher or controller. Candidate owners and exact interfaces/permissions are UNKNOWN until inspected. B1 protected-history ownership is separate. DT-IP-0003 covers revision-bound consumption receipts; DT-IP-0005 covers run freshness; DT-IP-0016 covers filesystem check/use races; DT-IP-0030 covers destination fencing. This prompt adds **cross-plane causal-attribution abstention**, not another receipt system or stale-writer fix.
