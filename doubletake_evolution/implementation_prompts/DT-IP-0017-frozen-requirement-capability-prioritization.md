## Distinct technical hypothesis and interfaces
Separate **task-specified reusable procedural requirements** from **observed current-skill outcomes**. Before examining results, freeze a capability vocabulary and task×capability incidence matrix from task contracts; do not substitute topic similarity or retrospectively assign labels to explain failures. For each capability, compute its task support fraction and observed success over training tasks requiring it, then prioritize high-support unresolved capability mass (support × remaining failure fraction). Select evidence and proposed revision around the same capability. Keep the matrix fixed during an evaluation round; explicitly detect changed task/authority contracts and abstain or re-freeze in a separately authorized new round rather than silently changing labels. Compare paired **whole-skill** results on training and separate selection sets before retaining a revision; keep the final transfer test untouched until final evaluation. An improvement on the selected capability alone is insufficient if unrelated tasks regress.

Potential owners/consumers, all **HYPOTHESES**: Director-approved task/subtask evaluator, capability-gap assessment, evidence selector, existing governed candidate validation. Do not create a registry, new prompt-mutation system, autonomous authority, or new mission.
