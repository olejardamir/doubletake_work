## Distinct problem and scope
A qualified skill may be **retrieved (R)** without being **incorporated/loaded (I)** into the active solver; even a loaded skill may not be **applied (A)** to produce useful downstream behavior. Conversely, irrelevant skills may load unnecessarily. A store entry, discovery, visibility, mission receipt or self-report is not evidence of actual run-bound load or effective use. This intermediate selection/loading discriminator differs from IS-009 / DT-IP-0010 per-consumer negative transfer *after* supply and IS-006 / DT-IP-0007 causal downstream Ground. Do not duplicate those tests or claim existing DT lacks this seam.
Author-reported SRA-Bench: 5,400 instances, 636 gold skills, 25,626 distractors (26,262 total). BM25 top-50 Qwen3-4B load rates 21.8% gold-present vs 16.9% absent; these are model/benchmark observations, **not DT metrics**, and do not establish an expected DT threshold.
