## Problem and distinct mechanism
Different reports/agents/wordings may descend from one evidence root, whereas independent roots can produce similar text. Report count, model count, semantic similarity and blanket one-root-one-vote are each insufficient as universal evidential weight. Same-root extracts may reveal complementary facts but saturate; common model errors can correlate across reports even with distinct roots. A content-only aggregator cannot universally distinguish cloned from independent evidence (two-report counterexample). This is **not** IS-009/DT-IP-0010 per-consumer negative transfer, IS-012/DT-IP-0013 tool-argument taint, or IS-010/011 write-history consistency.
