## Unique hypothesis and precise proof boundary
**H-007:** an eligible, genuinely novel source-derived rule **K = RFC 9162 append-only Merkle consistency verification** can, through an *existing authorized* DT knowledge-intake and consumer path, improve independent decisions on unseen synthetic old/new checkpoint-and-proof cases. Compare against a frozen pre-K baseline and source-withheld/wrong-source controls. **Do not assume novelty or a real consumer.**
- Verify independent anchoring of the *old* checkpoint and correct log identity, size, root and hash algorithm/domain separation; validate new checkpoint and consistency proof according to RFC §§2.1.1–2.1.4, including power-of-two and non-power-of-two sizes. Reject or abstain on malformed proof, wrong size/identity/root or unauthenticated checkpoint; distinguish invalid from unavailable evidence.
- A valid consistency proof supports append-only extension **of committed leaves under cryptographic assumptions only**. It does **not** prove event truth, completeness of submissions, signer authorization, process execution, persistence, liveness, or resistance to split-view/equivocation without independent checkpoint exchange. A stale valid head does not show fresh progress; a new unanchored head is UNKNOWN. Derived-map correctness requires stronger audit than log consistency.
- B1 use is **read-only owner-reviewed synthetic/expressly authorized diagnostic evidence**, never rewriting, re-sealing, truncating, repairing or bypassing protected history; never promote hash agreement to owner authority. An existing DT digest guard may already be sufficient. No new production CT server, database, network, writer, signer, privileged host change, or duplicate evaluator.
